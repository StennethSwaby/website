A Little About Me

My Journey

My professional journey began in the United States Navy, where I served as a Hospital Corpsman. During my service, I developed a strong appreciation for leadership, teamwork, adaptability, and the importance of working under pressure.

After transitioning from active-duty military service, I pursued a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at The College of New Jersey, along with a minor in Chinese. During my undergraduate studies, I explored software development and robotics, including research involving autonomous navigation and Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM).

I am currently pursuing a Master of Science in Engineering Management at the University of Massachusetts Lowell. My goal is to combine my technical background with business, leadership, and project management skills.

Outside of my professional interests, I enjoy aviation, learning new technologies, and exploring different cultures and languages.
